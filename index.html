About Me

Dr. Emmanuel Chinyere Echeonwu is an AI software engineer and researcher, known for pioneering SSOT-Centric Agentic Software Engineering frameworks and for his contributions in advancing engineering intelligence and artifact governance in AI-native systems.

His work bridges agentic software engineering with practical AI applications, focusing on building self-evolving, intelligent systems that integrate spatial intelligence, distributed systems, and AI-driven architecture patterns.

Dr. Echeonwu is the founder and CEO of OVUNS.TECH, an AI-first Software Engineering Research & Development startup dedicated to building software platforms powered by Engineering-SSOT, Agentic Software Engineering, and Engineering Intelligence.

His research portfolio spans agentic software systems, emergent AI behaviors, knowledge preservation in software evolution, geospatial intelligence, and intelligent artifact governance.
