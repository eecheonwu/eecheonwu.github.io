About Me

Dr. Emmanuel Chinyere Echeonwu is an AI software engineer and researcher, known for pioneering SSOT-Centric Agentic Software Engineering frameworks and for his contributions to intelligent systems research.

His work bridges agentic software engineering with practical AI applications, focusing on building self-evolving, intelligent systems that integrate spatial intelligence, distributed reasoning, and robust knowledge systems.

Dr. Echeonwu is the founder and CEO of OVUNS.TECH, an AI-first Software Engineering Research & Development startup dedicated to building software platforms that transform how organizations engineer and evolve digital systems.

His research portfolio spans agentic software systems, emergent AI behaviors, knowledge preservation in software evolution, geospatial intelligence, and intelligent artifacts.
